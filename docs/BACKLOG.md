# Backlog

Ideas for later, in priority order. Remove an item once it is built.

1. **Forecast scenarios:** "what if" toggles on top of the forecast, without saving. For example: no consulting income from a given month, another day rate, or another salary.
2. **Late consulting payments in the range:** widen the forecast's range for a consulting payment that slips into the next month.
3. **Two contracts under one merchant:** let recurring detection tell apart two payments with the same merchant and kind, such as two insurance policies.
4. **Everything together:** one combined view of Personal, Business and Kids. It cancels out the money moving between them, and shows total wealth and its forecast in one line.
5. **Year-end pack for the accountant:** export one year per entity as CSV or PDF, with:
   - the totals per category
   - the VAT paid against the VAT on income
   - the salary
   - the reimbursements
6. **Import reminders tied to the forecast:** when the latest import is older than an expected payment, say "import to confirm" instead of assuming the payment is still to come.
